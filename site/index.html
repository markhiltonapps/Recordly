Recordly Free and open source · AGPL-3.0

★ Screen recorder · Live ★

Record the screen. Polish the take.

Recordly is a desktop screen recorder and editor for walkthroughs, demos and product videos. It adds zooms, cursor polish and styled backgrounds without a motion designer.

★ Features · Live in 1.4.0 ★

From raw take to finished video.

One free app covers capture, a timeline editor, cursor and webcam styling, framing and export.

Record

Display · Window · Mic · System audio

  • Record a whole display or a single app window.
  • Capture your microphone and system audio.
  • Go straight from recording into the editor.
  • On Windows, a native Windows Graphics Capture helper and native WASAPI audio.

Edit

Trim · Zoom · Speed · Annotate

  • A drag-and-drop timeline. Trim the parts you don't want.
  • Draw zoom regions yourself, or take automatic suggestions based on cursor activity.
  • Speed-up and slow-down regions, plus extra audio regions.
  • Text, image and figure annotations. Crop the frame.
  • Save and reopen .recordly project files with the editor state kept.

Cursor

Size · Smoothing · Blur · Bounce

  • Show or hide the rendered cursor.
  • Set its size, smoothing, motion blur, click bounce and sway.
  • Loop mode for cleaner looping exports.
  • macOS-style cursor assets.

Webcam

Bubble · Mirror · Place · Scale

  • A webcam bubble that can be mirrored.
  • Set its size, pick a preset position or place it by X/Y.
  • Adjust margin, roundness and shadow.
  • Optional scaling that reacts to zoom.

Frame

Wallpaper · Padding · Corners · Ratio

  • Built-in wallpapers, your own uploaded backgrounds, solid colours and gradients.
  • Padding, rounded corners, background blur and drop shadows.
  • Aspect ratio presets.

Export

MP4 · GIF · Quality · Size

  • Export to MP4 or GIF, and choose the quality.
  • GIF frame rate, loop toggle and size presets.
  • Control the output dimensions.
  • Reveal the finished file in your system's file manager.

Shortcuts

Keyboard shortcuts are customisable, and the app has a built-in shortcut reference.

★ How it works · Three steps ★

Record. Edit. Export.

  1. 01

    Record

    Pick a whole display or one app window. Recordly can capture your mic and system audio. When you stop, the recording opens in the editor.

    Display or window · Mic + system audio
  2. 02

    Edit

    Trim the dead air. Accept the suggested zooms or draw your own. Speed up the slow parts, tidy the cursor and set the background.

    Zoom · Trim · Speed · Cursor
  3. 03

    Export

    Choose MP4 or GIF, set the quality and size, and export. Recordly can show you the file in your file manager when it's done.

    MP4 or GIF · Your dimensions
